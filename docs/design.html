Anatomy

Four things you read from one shape

  • Center glyphThe next ability to press. Seals sit in a hexagon frame so you can tell them from attacks at a glance. A number on the glyph means it's coming off cooldown.
  • Three inner arcsUnsealed Wrath stacks. They take the color of the stored seal: red for Command, amber for Righteousness.
  • Outer ringYour swing timer. It fills toward the notch at the top, and your next swing lands at the notch. When it's time to swap seals, the swap has to happen before the ring reaches the notch.
  • Two trailing orbsWhat's coming up next, fading with distance. Useful for planning a seal swap around the global cooldown.
All artwork is drawn from scratch in one line style: no game icons, no art borrowed from other addons. In-game, the halo is a single movable frame you can scale between 60% and 160%.

What the halo looks like when it matters

How it picks

Priority, top to bottom

Every moment, Sigil walks down this list and shows the first thing you can press. The order follows the current WoW Forever guides, adjusted for your talents. The guides disagree on whether Holy Strike or Judgement goes first; since Judgement skips the global cooldown, the order between those two barely matters.

    The weave

    One seal cycle, swing by swing

    Unsealed Wrath stores a copy of your seal on every swing that lands, up to three, for 15 seconds. A swing that lands while a different seal is active releases all stored copies at once. So you build three Command stacks, swap to Righteousness, let one swing cash them in, and swap back.

    Swing 1

    Command active. Stores Command 1/3.

    Swing 2

    Stores Command 2/3.

    Swing 3

    Stores Command 3/3. Halo pulses: swap.

    Seal of Righteousness
    Swing 4

    Releases 3 Command procs, each with its own chance to hit. Stores Righteousness 1/3.

    Seal of Command
    Swing 5

    Releases 1 Righteousness. Stores Command 1/3. Repeat.

    Seals cost a global cooldown. With a slow two-hander there's room for it; with a fast weapon Sigil starts calling the swap earlier so the global cooldown doesn't eat the window. A miss or a dodge stores nothing, so Sigil only counts swings that land.

    Around the rotation

    The things you already do by hand

    These stay out of the main halo so it only ever shows one thing to press. They appear as small marks beside it, and only when they need you.

    • Retribution Aura and your blessingA small mark appears when your aura is off or your blessing has run out. Sigil remembers whether you last cast Blessing of Might or Blessing of Wisdom and reminds you of that one.
    • Running out of manaWhen you can no longer afford your next ability, Sigil stops calling Consecration first, then shows a low-mana mark. That's your moment to switch to Blessing of Wisdom; Sigil won't nag you to switch back.
    • Hammer of JusticeJudging Seal of Command on a stunned target deals double damage. When Hammer of Justice and Judgement are both ready and Command is up, a mark shows the combo: stun, then Judgement. It's a suggestion on the side, never in the main queue, since you may be saving the stun.

    Limits

    What the game lets an addon see

    WoW Forever runs on the modern game client. During combat it hides several values from every addon, so Sigil keeps its own count from what you cast and when your swings land.

    Sigil can read

    • Your own castsThis is how it knows which seal is up and when it runs out (30 seconds).
    • Your swingsExact timing everywhere. Whether a swing hit or missed is readable in the open world only.
    • Whether a spell is readyReady or not, and whether you have the mana for it.
    • Target typeUndead or Demon, for Exorcism. Readable in the open world; in dungeons Sigil goes by whether Exorcism is usable.

    Hidden in combat

    • Buffs and debuffsSigil counts Unsealed Wrath stacks itself, from your swings. In dungeons and raids the game also hides misses, so the count can run one ahead after a miss or dodge. It corrects itself when the stacks expire or the fight ends.
    • Target healthNo percentage. Sigil shows Hammer of Wrath when the game marks it usable.
    • Exact manaSigil only knows “enough for this spell” or “not enough”.
    • Cooldown secondsSigil times them itself from your casts.

    Is this allowed? I found no Blizzard rule for WoW Forever that forbids an addon from suggesting an ability, and several such addons are on CurseForge today. Blizzard's stated direction for this game client is that addons shouldn't make combat decisions for you, and they enforce it by hiding information, so a later patch could hide more. If that happens, Sigil gets less precise; it doesn't put your account at risk.

    What already exists

    Other Forever addons, and where Sigil differs

    I looked at the paladin and rotation addons on CurseForge. They all work the same way underneath, counting from your casts and swings. They differ in how much you have to read.

    • Hekili Forever, Rotation Forge, ForeCastGeneral rotation helpers for many classes. Rows or bars of the game's own spell icons, with glows and colored borders. ForeCast and Hekili both handle the Unsealed Wrath swap for Retribution.
    • Paladin Seal Reminders, SwedgeTimer Forever, iSealTwistSeal and swing trackers. They show the stack count and a “twist” prompt, but not the rest of the rotation.
    • SigilOne spec and one shape: next ability, stacks and swing timer in a single halo, with its own drawn glyphs in place of game icons. It also covers your aura, blessing and Hammer of Justice habits.

    You asked for nothing that looks like PallyPower. That addon is a column of small buttons with the game's class and blessing icons on a tooltip-style backdrop. Sigil uses none of that: no button grid, no game icons, no stock backdrop.

    Macro

    The Attack macro

    One macro replaces four buttons. Each press tries your attacks from top to bottom and casts the first one that's ready. Make it under Escape → Macros, then drag it onto key 1.

    Attack

    #showtooltip
/startattack
/cast Judgement
/cast Hammer of Wrath
/cast Holy Strike
/cast Exorcism

    Judgement skips the global cooldown, so it fires together with the next attack. Exorcism only goes off on Undead and Demons. Hammer of Wrath does nothing until you learn it at 44.

    Test it first

    This macro relies on the game moving to the next line when a spell is on cooldown. Three WoW Forever macro sites publish a Judgement + Holy Strike macro built the same way and describe that behaviour, but none of them says it was tested in the live game.

    Hit a mob for 30 seconds while spamming it. If Judgement and Holy Strike both keep firing, it works. If only Judgement fires, tell me and I'll split it into two keys.

    Install

    Getting it into the game

    1. Download the Sigil folder I'll build.
    2. In CurseForge, open WoW Forever and use the folder button to open your AddOns folder (it sits under _forever_).
    3. Drop Sigil in there next to your other addons, then restart the game or type /reload.

    CurseForge will show Sigil but won't update it, since it isn't published there. If you like it, I can publish it so updates arrive through the app.
